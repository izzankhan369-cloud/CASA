Explore the Area

What's nearby

Casa de Goa puts you at the centre of everything North Goa has to offer — world-famous beaches, historic landmarks, vibrant markets and excellent food.

🏖

Baga Beach

Beach · ~10 min drive

One of North Goa's most iconic beaches with water sports, shacks, nightlife, and beautiful sunsets. A perennial favourite among visitors to the region.

🌊

Calangute Beach

Beach · ~12 min drive

Known as the "Queen of Beaches", Calangute is Goa's most popular stretch of sand — lively, vibrant and lined with restaurants and shops.

🌴

Anjuna Beach

Beach · ~15 min drive

Famous for its Wednesday flea market and bohemian atmosphere. Rocky outcrops, cliff-side cafes, and spectacular sunsets make Anjuna unmissable.

🛍

Anjuna Flea Market

Market · Every Wednesday

The legendary Anjuna Flea Market runs every Wednesday and is a treasure trove of handicrafts, clothing, spices, jewellery and Goan souvenirs.

🏰

Chapora Fort

History · ~20 min drive

The iconic Portuguese fort made famous by Bollywood. Panoramic views of Vagator Beach and the Arabian Sea from its ancient battlements are breathtaking.

🌅

Vagator Beach

Beach · ~18 min drive

A quieter, more dramatic beach flanked by red cliffs and backed by Chapora Fort. Ideal for those seeking natural beauty away from the larger beach crowds.

🌿

Spice Plantations

Nature · ~35 min drive

Walk through working spice farms growing cardamom, turmeric, vanilla and pepper. Guided tours offer insight into Goa's agricultural heritage with a traditional lunch.

✈️

GOI Airport

Transport · ~45 min drive

Goa's Manohar International Airport (Dabolim) is approximately 45 minutes away. Pre-booked taxis and app cabs are available; we can assist in arranging pickup.

🚂

Thivim Station

Transport · ~20 min drive

The nearest railway station for guests arriving by train. Thivim connects directly to major Indian cities. Taxis from the station to Casa de Goa take around 20 minutes.

Getting to Casa de Goa

✈️

By Air

Fly into Goa's Manohar International Airport (GOI). Take a pre-paid taxi or OLA/Uber to Casa de Goa — approximately 45 minutes, ₹600–800.

🚂

By Train

Arrive at Thivim Railway Station (North Goa). Taxis are readily available outside the station — the journey to Casa de Goa takes approximately 20 minutes.

🚗

By Road

Driving from Mumbai takes approximately 10–12 hours via NH66. From Pune, allow 8–9 hours. Ample parking is available at the property.
