Before You Book

Frequently asked questions

Does Casa de Goa require prepayment? +
No. Casa de Goa does not require any prepayment whatsoever. You confirm your booking via WhatsApp or email, and pay the full amount in cash or UPI at the property on arrival. No deposit, no hold, no stress.
Do I need a credit card to book? +
Not at all. Casa de Goa is one of the rare pay at property hotels in North Goa that requires absolutely no credit card details. Book via WhatsApp, confirm your dates, and pay when you arrive.
What are the check-in and check-out times? +
Standard check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out may be available subject to room availability — just ask via WhatsApp before your arrival.
What rooms are available and what are the prices? +
Casa de Goa offers three room types: Deluxe Double at ₹1,529/night, King Room at ₹1,619/night, and Deluxe Twin at ₹1,709/night. All rooms include AC, private balcony, and access to the private pool. See the rooms page for full details.
Is the swimming pool private? +
Yes. The pool at Casa de Goa is exclusively for guests staying at the property — not shared with the public or other hotel complexes. This is a genuine private pool experience in North Goa.
How far is Casa de Goa from the beach? +
Casa de Goa is approximately 10 minutes from Baga Beach and 12 minutes from Calangute Beach by car. Anjuna and Vagator are around 15–18 minutes away. Two-wheelers and taxis are easily available from the property. See our location page for a full guide.
